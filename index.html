LIFE AFTER CAMPUS

A story about success, choices, faith and values

After graduation, Vincent and Annastacia entered the job market with the same dream: to build successful lives. Months passed, but neither could find a stable job. Vincent survived through small jobs while Annastacia struggled with rent, family pressure and constant rejection.

Eventually, Annastacia got a job at a growing company. Vincent congratulated her, even as he quietly wondered why his own prayers seemed unanswered. Annastacia encouraged him not to measure his worth by the speed at which opportunities came.

At work, Annastacia discovered that success sometimes came with difficult choices. Her supervisor expected her to overlook questionable practices to help the company meet its targets. She feared that speaking up might cost her the job she had worked so hard to get.

Vincent, meanwhile, continued applying for jobs and improving his skills. Some days he felt forgotten, especially when he saw former classmates announcing promotions and new opportunities online. He began to wonder whether living by his values was worth it when dishonest people appeared to be moving ahead.

When the two met again, Annastacia shared her struggle. She realized that having a job did not automatically mean having peace. Vincent listened and reminded her of the values they had discussed while on campus. Their conversation helped him see that waiting for an opportunity did not mean he had to abandon his principles or stop preparing for the future.

Annastacia decided to seek advice from someone she trusted and look for an honest way to address the situation at work. The decision was not easy, and she did not know how her employer would respond. Still, she understood that her integrity mattered more than a quick reward.

Both began to understand that life after campus was not simply about earning money, gaining titles or proving themselves to others. It was also about patience, responsibility, faith and the courage to make choices they could live with. Their paths were different, but each had to decide what kind of person they wanted to become.

Scripture for Reflection

Read these passages and consider how they relate to the story.

Reflection & Discussion

Take your time. Listen respectfully, challenge ideas thoughtfully and allow everyone to contribute.

1Annastacia's Choices

Was Annastacia right to question the practices at her workplace, even though her job was at risk? What should guide a Christian when doing the right thing comes at a personal cost?

2Honesty and Patience

Vincent struggled while watching other people succeed. How can someone remain patient and honest when dishonest people appear to be progressing faster? Is patience enough, or must it be accompanied by action?

3What Is Success?

How should Christians define success after campus? If someone has money, status and a good job but lacks peace or integrity, should that person be considered successful? Why?

4When Values Are Tested

Imagine your employer asks you to do something dishonest and warns that refusing may cost you your job. What would you do? What practical steps could help you stand by your values without acting recklessly?

5Protecting Faith and Identity

How can a young Catholic maintain faith, identity and healthy relationships while facing unemployment, family expectations, financial pressure and competition after campus? What support should the Church community provide?

Discussion reminder: There may be different perspectives. Explain your reasoning, refer to Scripture where helpful, and respect others as you discuss.
