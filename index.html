LIFE AFTER CAMPUS

A story about success, choices, faith and values

After graduation, Vincent and Annastacia entered the job market with the same dream: to build successful lives. Months passed, but neither could find a stable job. Vincent survived through small jobs while Annastacia struggled with rent, family pressure, and constant rejection.

Eventually, Annastacia got a job at a big company. Her boss made it clear that her career would move faster if she was willing to give him more than her work. Desperate to succeed, Annastacia made choices that helped her climb the corporate ladder, even though they conflicted with her values.

Vincent, meanwhile, remained at the bottom. He watched his classmates succeed and began wondering whether honesty and patience were simply making him lose in life. Sometimes, he questioned God, wondering why his prayers seemed unanswered while others appeared to be moving forward.

As the years passed, Annastacia's position and income grew. However, the decisions she had made continued to trouble her. Vincent's journey was slower, but he kept learning, taking opportunities where he could, and holding onto his principles. Neither of them had the perfect life they imagined when they graduated.

Eventually, they met again and talked about how different their lives had become. They realized that campus had prepared them for careers, but not necessarily for the difficult choices that came afterward. They began to understand that success is not only about how far you go, but also about who you become along the way.

DISCUSSION QUESTIONS

Let's talk, listen and learn from one another

01

Understanding Annastacia's Choices

Was Annastacia completely wrong for the choices she made, considering the financial and social pressure she was under? Where should we draw the line between understanding someone's circumstances and excusing their actions?

02

Honesty and Patience

Vincent began questioning whether honesty and patience were worth it. Have you ever felt that doing the right thing puts you at a disadvantage while others seem to succeed through shortcuts?

03

What Is Success?

What does “success” mean to a Christian after campus? Is it wealth, a good career, status, helping your family, personal happiness, faith—or a combination of these?

04

When Values Are Tested

If you were in Vincent's position and received an opportunity that required you to compromise your values, what factors would influence your decision? Is there ever a situation where compromising is justified?

05

Protecting Faith and Identity

Matthew 16:26 asks what it profits someone to gain the world but lose their soul. In today's competitive job market, what are young graduates most likely to sacrifice in pursuit of success—and how can they protect their faith, values, and identity?
