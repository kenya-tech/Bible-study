THIS WEEK'S BIBLE STUDY

LIFE AFTER CAMPUS

A story about faith, pressure, success and the choices we make after graduation.

After graduation, Vincent and Annastacia entered the job market with the same dream: to build successful lives. Months passed, but neither could find a stable job. Vincent survived through small jobs while Annastacia struggled with rent, family pressure, and constant rejection.

Every morning, Vincent would wake up early, dress for interviews, and send applications. Most days ended with no response. He started taking temporary jobs just to put food on the table. His friends seemed to be moving ahead, and every graduation photo or new-job announcement on social media made him question whether he was falling behind.

Annastacia's situation became even more difficult. Her rent was overdue, her family needed her support, and she was constantly being asked when she would finally get a “real job.” Eventually, she got an opportunity at a big company. For the first time in months, she felt that her life was finally changing.

However, her boss soon made it clear that her career would move faster if she was willing to give him more than her work. At first, Annastacia refused and tried to prove herself through hard work. But as the pressure increased, she began wondering whether her principles could survive in a world where people seemed willing to do anything to get ahead.

Eventually, Annastacia made choices that helped her climb the corporate ladder, even though they conflicted with her values. Her salary increased, her lifestyle changed, and people began admiring her success. From the outside, she appeared to have everything. But privately, she struggled with the weight of the decisions she had made to get there.

Vincent, meanwhile, remained at the bottom. He watched former classmates get better jobs while he continued taking small opportunities. There were moments when he wanted to give up and wondered whether honesty, patience, and prayer were actually enough. Yet he continued learning new skills, taking whatever honest opportunities came his way, and trusting that his journey did not have to look like everyone else's.

Years later, Vincent and Annastacia met again. They talked about their lives and realized that neither had experienced the future they had imagined on graduation day. Annastacia had money and a powerful position, but carried regrets she rarely spoke about. Vincent had taken longer to build his career, but he was proud that he had not completely lost himself along the way.

They both learned that life after campus is not simply about finding a job or becoming successful. It is about facing pressure, making difficult choices, dealing with failure, and deciding what you are willing to sacrifice to become the person you want to be.

DISCUSSION

Reflection Questions

Take time to think deeply about the story before discussing the questions together.

01

Understanding Annastacia's Choices

Was Annastacia completely wrong for the choices she made considering the financial and social pressure she was under? Where is the line between understanding someone's circumstances and excusing their actions?

02

When Doing Right Feels Like Losing

Vincent questioned whether honesty and patience were actually enough. Have you ever felt that doing the right thing puts you at a disadvantage while others seem to succeed through shortcuts?

03

What Does Success Really Mean?

What should success mean to a Christian after campus? Is it wealth, career, status, helping family, happiness, faith, or a combination of these?

04

How Much Should We Compromise?

If you were in Vincent's position and an opportunity required you to compromise your values, what factors would influence your decision? Is compromise ever justified?

05

Protecting Your Identity

Matthew 16:26 asks what it profits someone to gain the whole world but lose their soul. In today's competitive job market, what are young graduates most likely to sacrifice, and how can they protect their faith, values and identity?

Catholic Prayers

Prayer for Guidance

Lord, guide us as we make decisions about our future. Give us wisdom when opportunities come our way and courage to choose what is right even when the easier path is tempting.

Help us to trust Your timing and to remember that our worth is not determined by our careers, income or achievements. May our choices always bring us closer to You.

Amen.
🙏

Prayer for Young Graduates

Heavenly Father, be with every young person entering the world after campus. Open doors that are meant for us and give us patience when those doors seem slow to open.

Protect us from desperation, dishonesty and temptation. Help us to work hard, remain faithful and trust that You have a purpose for every season of our lives.

Amen.
🕊

Prayer for Strength

Lord Jesus, when we feel behind, rejected or discouraged, remind us that our journey is different from everyone else's.

Give us strength to keep going, humility to learn, courage to begin again and faith to believe that You are working even when we cannot see the outcome.

Amen.
